Hebbian Plasticity + Synaptic Ultrastructure + Receptor Ultrastructure

Every intersection has the same NMDA receptors throughout — they never change. What grows with each co-activation is the AMPA receptor count: weight 0 has none, weight 1 adds one AMPA, weight 2 adds two AMPA.

When a neuron fires, membrane depolarisation ejects the Mg²⁺ ion that normally blocks the NMDA channel pore. This unblocking is shown by the gold Mg²⁺ ion leaving the channel during activation.
