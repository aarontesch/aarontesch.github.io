    What the model is doing

    Shaping is a learning model: each behavior class has a response strength, a pellet raises the strength of whatever class the rat was in when it arrived, discounted by the delay, and unreinforced strength decays. Nothing about lever pressing is given to the rat in advance — a student who reinforces badly gets no pressing at all.

    Schedules work differently. The response is already established by that point, so each schedule drives a scripted performance profile chosen to reproduce its documented signature: response rate, post-reinforcement pause, and resistance to extinction. This is a demonstration of the patterns, not a derivation of them.
