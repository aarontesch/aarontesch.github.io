How to play

Name a career goal for your child, then work through five developmental stages. At each one you decide how to parent: carpentry, which points that stage of their development at the goal, or gardening, which supports whatever they are growing into. You only get five stages, and a stage can only be one or the other. Play again to see how a different mix of the two lands.
Occupation figures from BLS 2025 and Doximity 2026, expressed as percentiles. The game is a thought experiment, not a model of real children.
