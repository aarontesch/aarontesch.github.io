The Eight Crises

You start as an infant and live one life, forward, through all eight of Erikson's stages. Each crisis resolves as a ratio — not a win. What you carry out of one stage is what you bring into the next.

Epigenetic chart
Stage 1 of 8 — filled blue where the syntonic tendency carried, red where the dystonic did.

Terms used here are Erikson's: each stage pairs a syntonic and a dystonic tendency, and healthy resolution is a favorable ratio between them — never the elimination of the dystonic pole. Successful resolution yields a basic virtue; failure yields what Erikson called a core pathology or antipathy. Stage ages are approximate and culturally variable; Erikson revised them himself. Primary sources: Childhood and Society (1950), Identity: Youth and Crisis (1968), and The Life Cycle Completed (1982, with Joan Erikson).
